Is it AI?

AI · Level 1
Unit 1 · Lesson 1
Time45 minutes
Ages10–15 (adaptable)
MaterialsBoard, chalk, 12 paper cards. No devices or electricity needed.

Learning goal

Pupils can explain that AI learns patterns from examples, and can tell AI tools apart from ordinary machines.

Steps

  1. Warm-up: calculator or assistant?

    Ask: “What can a calculator do? What can a phone assistant do?” List answers in two columns on the board.
    5 min

  2. Human or machine?

    Read 6 short sentences aloud. Pupils stand up if they think a machine wrote it. Discuss: how could a machine learn to write like that?
    10 min

  3. Unplugged game: teach the machine

    One pupil is “the model”. Show 8 cards labelled “fruit” or “not fruit”. Then show 4 new unlabelled cards — the model must guess. Discuss wrong guesses: were the examples enough?
    20 min

  4. Exit ticket

    Pupils complete question 4 of the worksheet and hand it in.
    10 min

Key words

artificial intelligence (AI)dataexamplepatternpredictionmodel

Try this tomorrow

Keep the cards. In the next lesson, give the “model” only examples of red fruit and ask it about a green apple. This shows how biased examples cause wrong answers.
