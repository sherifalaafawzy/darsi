# Darsi German glossary (de, "du")

One German term per concept. The app pack (`content/i18n/de.json`) is the authority; courses, kits, staff screens
and Labs follow it. Register: "du", never "Sie". "KI" for AI. Gender-fair where natural: Lehrkraft, Lernende,
Studierende, and the colon form for pupils (Schüler:innen). Professions in running text may use full pairs
(Ärztinnen und Ärzte) or the colon form, but pupils and students are always Schüler:innen / Studierende.
